# 2026-07-17 session G — the polish session: lit-silk wallpapers, the comet ring, the Hero Clock

The owner asked for a bigger, more modern brand presence across the doorway
surfaces and the desktop: no cheap-looking art anywhere, a larger and more
distinctive mark in the bar, per-theme designed wallpapers (Canva if
possible), a new desktop widget, and clock polish — then push, update the
machine, rebuild the ISO. Everything below is on `main`; every new gate was
broken once on purpose and watched go red.

## What landed

1. **Wallpapers v2 — lit silk.** `make_wallpaper` in
   `artwork/generate_moos_themes.py` was rewritten. v1's single crest lines
   filled to the bottom edge read as line-art next to the painterly Graphite
   master. v2 keeps the deterministic pure-PIL contract and changes how the
   light works: silk bands between two curves, gradient-lit along their
   length and lit FROM the crest (the theme's light colour breathing down
   into the glass), inner shadows, filaments, screen-blended neon crest
   edges, an aurora veil, a soft beam, bokeh (Amethyst), a razor light-arc
   (Midnight OLED), star fields with cross-flares. **Canva was retried and
   the account's AI quota is still hard-blocked** (same error as
   2026-07-16); the generator ships the art, Canva can replace the masters
   later without touching packaging.

2. **The comet ring.** `comet_ring()` in `generate_login_scene.py` bakes a
   new sprite: a thin luminous arc, cyan head → violet tail, fading along
   250°. Rotating it reads as an orbit with direction (a uniform circle
   reads as a static border). It now orbits the emblem on every doorway:
   login (two counter-rotating rings + cinematic scale-settle entrance +
   a spark gliding the hairline + six drifting motes), lock (one mirrored
   ring), logout (ring atop the glass panel; the watermark breathes), the
   bar, the glance popup, and the Hero Clock.

3. **The bar brand grew.** Compact slot 1.2×→1.5× of panel height; the ring
   orbits continuously, sized off the PANEL height so it never clips;
   brighter under the pointer.

4. **MoOS Hero Clock** (`org.moos.heroclock`) — a new first-party desktop
   widget: large Light time + breathing colon + live seconds, bilingual
   dates, the accent hairline with the gliding spark, and the living mark in
   the corner of a glass card. **Trap found live:** fonts sized off height
   alone pushed the seconds strip clean out of the card on a square window
   (plasmawindowed hands you one) — every size now derives from
   `Math.min(width, height)`. Verified live at two aspects; shadow copies
   deleted afterwards.

5. **Clock polish.** The lock clock's turquoise tick breathes in period with
   the brand; the panel clock (nova.clock) sweeps a turquoise hairline in
   under the time on hover.
